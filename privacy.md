# HP-LQC Research Backup — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

## 1. Overview

HP-LQC Research Backup is a research backup utility developed to preserve notebooks and supporting files associated with the Holographic-Primordial Loop Quantum Cosmology (HP-LQC) research project.

The application uses Google OAuth authorization and Google Drive to maintain backups of research files stored in a private GitHub repository.

## 2. Information Accessed

When authorized, the application may access Google Drive files and folders as permitted by the OAuth permissions granted by the user.

The application processes research files and associated metadata as necessary to perform backup operations.

The exact scope of access depends on the permissions granted during authorization.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used to:

- Upload and synchronize research backup files.
- Maintain current copies of research notebooks.
- Preserve previous file versions when backup files are replaced.
- Verify backup integrity and support recovery.

Google Drive information is not used for advertising or marketing.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Research backup files are stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the user.

Backup operations are executed using GitHub Actions and rclone.

OAuth credentials are stored as GitHub Actions secrets for automated authentication.

The application does not intentionally make private research files publicly available or sell user data.

Data is processed by GitHub and Google as necessary to operate the backup workflow, subject to their respective services and policies.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until they are deleted by the account owner or through an authorized backup-management operation.

Historical backup copies may remain after newer versions are uploaded.

The account owner can delete backup files directly from Google Drive.

## 6. Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google Account access through:

https://myaccount.google.com/connections

Revoking access prevents further authorized Google Drive operations using that connection. Existing backup files are not automatically deleted.

## 7. Security

The application uses OAuth authorization rather than storing the user's Google account password.

Access credentials are intended to remain confidential and must not be included in public repositories or research documents.
